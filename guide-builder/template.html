Read first: what the official docs don't say clearly

  • The X/Y motors stay unplugged from {a:securing-the-bed-spacer-right-additional-information} until {a:mounting-the-left-cover-covering-the-electronics} (after the heatbed alignment). Several commenters suggest plugging them back in earlier, around {s:1102169}. That isn't in the official instructions; if you do it, check the X/Y connections in that final article section.
  • Three things stay loose until the Gen 2 heatbed alignment ({s:1110993..1111043}): the heatbed screws (two turns only in {s:1099926}), the expansion joints' final alignment, and the offset-sensor screw (a few turns in {s:1099523}).
  • Use the new 10 mm heatbed spacer ({s:1110975}), not the old 8 mm one that {s:1099715} lists.
  • Skip {s:1116271} (tensioner lube is already done) and {s:1113247} (INDX doesn't use the Bowden-guide).
  • Gen 2 edition change: the Gen 2 guide requires setting the printer edition to Gen 2 because of the new belt pitch. The companion article never mentions it. It's included right after {s:1109709}, with a heads-up on that step itself, because that's where you pick the printer model. On the compiler's printer, set up with firmware 6.9.2, the edition was already set to Gen 2 INDX, so it was only a check.
  • From comments: many people found it easier to take both side panels off early and leave them off until needed. Commenters also recommend having M3x12 screws for the head cable clip ({s:1102054}), and pre-threading the self-tapping holes in printed parts throughout chapter 5.
